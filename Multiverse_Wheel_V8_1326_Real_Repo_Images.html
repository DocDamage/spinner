How This Version Works

Real roster and full kits

The origin, opponents, recruits, mentors, transformations, and many artifacts use named heroes and villains. Open any dossier to see every power included in that exact gameplay profile. All listed stats, powers, tags, forms, party support, and artifacts feed the battle calculation.

Wheel balance

Normal wheels now use six dynamic world archetypes: Battle, Treasure, Crisis, Tournament, Training, and Multiverse Collision. Each creates a different 14-slice mix of battles, missions, power sets, transformations, recruits, artifacts, recovery, rare events, and at most one hazard where that world calls for it. Hazard cooldown still prevents repeated hazard spam.

Battle choices

  • Power Clash favors Might, Defense, and Energy.
  • Speed Blitz favors Speed, Skill, and prediction.
  • Tactical Counter favors Mind, Skill, preparation, and technology.
  • Mystic / Hax favors magic, reality, soul, time, and space powers.
  • Outlast favors Defense, healing, regeneration, and adaptation.

Power growth

Landing on a character power set adds that character's entire listed kit. Winning a battle also lets you copy the opponent's complete kit, recruit them, or convert the victory into raw stat growth.

Saving

The run autosaves locally after every decision. The New button erases the current run only after confirmation.
