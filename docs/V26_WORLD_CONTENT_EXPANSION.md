# Multiverse Wheel V26 — World Content Expansion

## Release intent

V26 is the content-density release. V16–V25 already provide the living worlds, economy, party consequences, relics, factions, strongholds, settlements, tactical operations, activities, and crisis arcs. V26 does not add another competing simulator. It gives those systems a much broader visual vocabulary and a player-facing **World Asset Atlas**.

The release ships **376 rights-safe original project assets** across eleven families and assigns them deterministically to the existing game state. They are real SVG scene/icon assets produced from the catalog at runtime, not remote image hotlinks and not mislabeled franchise substitutions.

## Asset families

- **48 buildings** — clinics, market halls, academies, archives, forgeworks, transit depots, sanctuaries, and arenas across six visual themes.
- **48 places** — downtown sectors, forests, storm coasts, wastelands, mountains, space stations, hidden temples, and fracture zones.
- **30 interiors** — war rooms, medical bays, workshops, vaults, hangars, and common halls.
- **60 items** — weapons, shields, gadgets, healing kits, transformation catalysts, relic fragments, provisions, batteries, siege supplies, and recovery supplies.
- **40 vehicles** — cars, bikes, cargo vehicles, rescue carriers, APCs, VTOL craft, starfighters, and dimensional vessels.
- **30 NPC roles** — medics, quartermasters, engineers, operatives, scavengers, and event hosts.
- **20 stronghold visuals** — command bastions, relic citadels, portal fortresses, research keeps, and refuge strongholds.
- **20 settlement visuals** — stable, strained, rebuilding, and fortified civilian districts.
- **20 activity visuals** — raceways, tournament arenas, relic trials, hunt courses, and rescue drill zones.
- **20 crisis visuals** — fracture, corruption, invasion, temporal, refugee, stronghold, relic, and portal emergency scenes.
- **40 UI icons/badges** — battle, travel, relic, vehicle, stronghold, civilian, crisis, and reward markers.

Total: **376** new world-content assets.

## Rights-safe art strategy

Character and transformation imagery is intentionally kept on Spinner's existing **review-first** identity-verification pipeline. V26 does not auto-download or guess copyrighted character/form art.

The new world-content families are different: they are **Spinner Original Multiverse** assets. Each record drives deterministic SVG project art generated by `js/world-asset-art.js`. No network call is required, the PWA can render them offline, and no external image needs to be cached.

This also prevents the repository/PWA-size problem that would come from committing hundreds of redundant 720×480 raster images. The catalog and renderer remain small while still producing hundreds of distinct visual assets.

## Formal asset metadata

Every V26 asset records:

- `id`
- `kind`
- `name`
- `subtype`
- `sourceFranchise`
- `world`
- `faction`
- `rarity`
- `tags`
- `path`
- `mime`
- `width`
- `height`
- `sha256`
- `sourcePage`
- `verified`
- `fallbackAllowed`
- `usageTargets`
- `notes`
- deterministic `visualSeed`

For runtime-generated V26 SVG assets, `path` uses `generated:v26/...`, `mime` is `image/svg+xml`, `sourcePage` is `generated-project-art`, `verified` is `true`, and `sha256` is deliberately `null` until an asset is materialized to bytes. The validator enforces that contract.

## Existing-system integration

V26 owns only visual catalog data, stable visual assignments, Atlas favorites/recent views, and Atlas settings. It does not duplicate any gameplay authority.

- **V16 worlds** receive stable place/building context art.
- **V21 strongholds/factions** receive stronghold, building, interior, vehicle, item, and NPC support art.
- **V22 settlements** receive settlement, building, place, NPC, and supply visuals.
- **V23 operations** receive mission locations, interiors, vehicles, support NPCs, and equipment visuals.
- **V24 activities** receive venue, race vehicle, event, and host visuals.
- **V25 crises** receive crisis scenes, affected places, response vehicles, supplies, and NPC support visuals.

Assignments are seeded and persisted under `state.v26.assignments`. Re-rendering a screen never rerolls its visual identity. V26 never mutates the authoritative V16–V25 records merely to choose art.

## World Asset Atlas

Open **World → Atlas** to browse the full library.

The Atlas provides:

- kind filters;
- rarity filter;
- text search across names, themes, tags, worlds, factions, and usage targets;
- lazy-rendered SVG art cards;
- favorites;
- bounded recent-view history;
- a 72-card render window so hundreds of assets do not flood the DOM at once.

A **World Asset Atlas** beacon shows total library size and high-value category counts.

## Context art

Existing World tabs gain a compact V26 context-art strip rather than a duplicate gameplay screen. The strip uses the saved assignment for the currently relevant world, stronghold, settlement, operation, activity, or crisis and provides a direct route to the Atlas.

This makes the content systemic: assets appear where the V16–V25 state says they matter.

## Persistence boundaries

V26 schema is `26`.

`state.v26` contains:

- `assignments`
- `favorites`
- `recent`
- `settings`
- `stats`

There is **no V26 wallet, currency, inventory, combat model, settlement simulator, faction model, operation model, activity model, or crisis model**.

Migration is idempotent and preserves V25 state.

## Offline/PWA behavior

The V26 service-worker cache precaches:

- `js/data/world-content.js`
- `js/world-asset-art.js`
- `js/domain/v26-engine.js`
- `js/v26-experience.js`
- `styles/v26.css`

The generated SVG scenes are data URIs produced locally from those cached files. First-install claim safety and explicit `SKIP_WAITING` update behavior remain unchanged.

## Performance design

V26 avoids a cache-size explosion in four ways:

1. the catalog is data, not hundreds of raster files;
2. visuals are generated only when rendered;
3. the Atlas displays at most 72 cards per view;
4. card images use browser lazy loading.

This gives Spinner substantially more visual content without making every launch download an enormous art pack.

## What V26 deliberately does not fake

V26 does not claim that the 2,346 transformation targets suddenly have verified exact franchise art. That remains a separate review queue because identity correctness matters more than filling a slot with the wrong picture. The V26 world-content expansion is fully usable now while character/transformation verification can continue safely in parallel.
